War in Middle Earth is a 1989 strategy game for MSX, on cassette. Two things built on it live here: a patch that fixes what hurt most when playing and finishes its translation into Spanish, and an enhanced cartridge that boots in nine seconds instead of six and a half minutes and adds music, new pictures and more speed.
